# A joint that didn't fit

The first dovetail I cut this year was loose enough to rattle. Here's how I made it worse before making it better.

## Mistake one

Sanded the pins to "fix" the gap. This just made a loose joint with rounded edges.

## Mistake two

Added glue and clamped hard, hoping pressure would hide the gap. It didn't; it just spread the glue line wider.

## What actually worked

Cut a new set of pins. Slower, but the only real fix. `measure twice` is a cliché because it's true.
