# What the tomatoes taught me

Three plants, none of them dead, which for me counts as a triumph.

## The lesson

I watered every day because I thought that was caring for them. It wasn't. Tomatoes want to be a little thirsty. ~~Water daily.~~ Water when the top inch of soil is dry, which turned out to be every three or four days here.

Next year: fewer plants, more restraint.
