# Venue

SignalConf 2026 runs at the **Lisbon Congress Centre**, a five minute walk
from Cais do Sodre station.

**Address**
Praca das Industrias, 1300-307 Lisbon, Portugal

## Getting there

- **From the airport:** the red metro line to Sao Sebastiao, then the green line to Cais do Sodre, about 35 minutes door to door.
- **By train:** Cais do Sodre station sits on the Cascais line and is a five minute walk from the venue.
- **Parking:** limited on-site parking is available; street parking nearby fills up early.

## Where to stay

There's no official conference hotel. Most attendees stay somewhere along
the Cais do Sodre to Chiado stretch, walking distance to both the venue
and the city center.
