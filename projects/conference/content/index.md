## About SignalConf

SignalConf is a small, focused conference for people who build and operate
distributed systems: queues, replication, consensus, and the failure modes
that come with them. No expo hall, no six parallel tracks, just two days of
talks worth your attention and enough breaks to actually talk to people.

## Why attend

- Four talks a day, picked for depth over breadth
- Every session is recorded and shared with attendees afterward
- Small enough that you will talk to every speaker, not just watch them leave the stage
